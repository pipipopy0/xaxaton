Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calendator ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the Calendator Telegram bot and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following information:

  • Telegram account information: Telegram User ID, name and username (if provided by Telegram), language, and other settings necessary for the Service to operate.
  • User content: text messages and commands sent to the Service, voice messages submitted for processing, voice message transcriptions, and information necessary to create, modify, and display calendar events.
  • Calendar data: event titles, start and end dates and times, event descriptions, information necessary for calendar synchronization, and other calendar information that you have authorized the Service to access.
  • Service settings: time zone, notification settings, subscription settings, and other user preferences.
  • Subscription and payment information: selected subscription plan, subscription status, subscription start and end dates, automatic renewal status, payment identifiers, payment status, and other technical information necessary to manage subscriptions and payments.
  • Support information: the content of your support requests, contact information you provide, and technical information necessary to resolve your request.
  • Technical information: information about your use of the Service, technical logs, error information, security-related information, and information about the costs of third-party APIs used for internal accounting and Service management.

Full bank card details are processed by the applicable payment provider and are not stored by Calendator where such information is transmitted directly to the payment provider.

2. Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Calendator may access calendar data within the permissions you grant through Google's authorization system.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include event information such as titles, descriptions, start and end dates and times, attendees, and other information necessary to provide the relevant functionality.

Google Calendar data is used to create, modify, synchronize, and display calendar events based on your requests.

Calendator uses data obtained through Google APIs only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant Service functionality and complies with applicable requirements of the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Calendator does not receive your Google account password. Authorization is performed using OAuth 2.0.

3. Apple Calendar

If you connect Apple Calendar (iCloud Calendar), synchronization may require an App-Specific Password created by you in your Apple account settings.

This password is used solely to connect to your calendar through the applicable synchronization protocol.

Calendator does not receive your primary Apple Account password.

Access is used only to the extent necessary to work with the calendar data that you have authorized the Service to synchronize.

You may terminate access to your calendar by revoking the relevant App-Specific Password or disabling the integration with the Service.

4. Third-Party Services and Artificial Intelligence

Certain Calendator features may use third-party services, including artificial intelligence, speech recognition, cloud hosting, payment processing, and other technical services.

Your text or voice data may be transmitted to the relevant provider when this is necessary to perform an operation requested by you.

Data is transmitted only to the extent necessary to perform the relevant operation.

The specific list of third-party providers may change as the Service develops.
